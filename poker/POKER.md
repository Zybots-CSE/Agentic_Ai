# No-Limit Texas Hold'em Rules (No Blinds)

## Tournament Structure
- 10 hands of poker
- Players are given 50k at the start of each hand
- Their winnings (ie. the un bet points + winnings) is added to a running score
- They cannot use winnings from previous games to bet in new games (they will always have 50k points at the beginning of a hand, no more, no less)
- The player with the highest winnings is the winner of the tournament
 
## Overview
- 52-card deck.
- 2-10 players.
- Dealer button rotates clockwise each hand.
- Two private hole cards per player.
- Five community cards.
- Best 5-card hand from any combination of 2 hole + 5 community cards.

## Forced Bets
- None. No blinds and no antes.
- The pot is built entirely through voluntary betting once action begins.
## Starting Position
- Players are seated clockwise starting from the dealer: Player 1 (dealer), Player 2, Player 3, and so on.
- Betting starts immediately after the hole cards are dealt, beginning with Player 1.
- Player 1 acts first and, since no bet has been made yet, may bet any amount (or check, since no forced bet exists).
- Action then proceeds clockwise: each subsequent player may fold, check (if no bet has been made), call, or raise.
- For all later rounds (flop, turn, river), action also starts with Player 1 (or the next active player clockwise from Player 1, if Player 1 has folded).
## Betting Structure
Four betting rounds:
1. Preflop
2. Flop
3. Turn
4. River
No-limit rules apply:
- A player may bet or raise any amount up to their entire remaining stack at any time.
- Minimum bet: no fixed minimum bet size is required by these rules; if a table minimum is desired, set it separately (e.g., equal to the smallest chip denomination in play).
- Minimum raise: a raise must be at least equal to the size of the previous bet or raise in that round (standard no-limit minimum-raise rule), unless a player is going all-in for less.
- No cap on the number of raises in a round.
Allowed actions:

- Fold
- Check (only if facing no bet)
- Call
- Bet (if no bet yet)
- Raise (any amount, subject to the minimum-raise rule above)
## Deal
1. Shuffle.
2. Deal two hole cards to each player (no blinds or antes posted).
3. Betting round (starts with Player 1).
4. Burn one card.
5. Deal Flop (3 cards).
6. Betting round (starts with Player 1, or next active player clockwise).
7. Burn one card.
8. Deal Turn (1 card).
9. Betting round.
10. Burn one card.
11. Deal River (1 card).
12. Betting round.
13. Showdown if needed.
## Betting Round Ends
Ends when:
- Every active player has acted.
- All active players have matched the highest wager or are all-in.

- No further raises occur.
## Showdown
- Remaining players reveal hole cards.
- Best 5-card poker hand wins.
- Split pot evenly on ties.
- Odd chip goes to first winner clockwise from dealer.
## Hand Rankings
1. Royal Flush
2. Straight Flush
3. Four of a Kind
4. Full House
5. Flush
6. Straight
7. Three of a Kind
8. Two Pair
9. One Pair
10. High Card
## All-In

- A player with insufficient chips to call may go all-in for whatever amount remains in their stack.
- Create main and side pots as needed.
- A player only competes for pots they contributed to.
## End of Hand
- Award pots.
- Move dealer button clockwise (the new Player 1 becomes the new first-to-act seat).
- Start next hand.
